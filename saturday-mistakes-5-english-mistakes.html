1. "I will be go" — Why is BE there?

SLIDE 01 / 05
❌ COMMON TRAPI will be go to the cinema.
✅ COSY CORRECTIONI will go to the cinema. OR I will be going to the cinema.
📐 COSY FORMULASubject + will + [Base Verb] (Simple Future)
Subject + will be + [Verb-ing] (Continuous Future)
💡 MEMORY HOOKIf you use be, the next verb MUST end in -ing! Never put be directly before a bare verb like go or eat.
📝 QUICK QUIZ: Which is correct?
A. I will be visit...
B. I will visit my parents tomorrow.
C. I will visiting...
Answer: B — Keep will + bare verb and will be + verb-ing in separate compartments!

2. "I spend time for learning English"

SLIDE 02 / 05
❌ COMMON TRAPI spend one hour for learning English every day.
✅ COSY CORRECTIONI spend one hour learning English every day.
📐 COSY FORMULASubject + spend + [Time / Money] + [Verb-ing]
💡 MEMORY HOOKDo not insert 'for' or 'to' after 'spend time'! The gerund (-ing) connects directly to the time phrase.
📝 QUICK QUIZ: Which is correct?
A. I spend thirty minutes to read...
B. I spend thirty minutes reading before bed.
C. I spend thirty minutes for read...
Answer: B — When you say spend time, jump straight to -ing!

3. "I have no some problems"

SLIDE 03 / 05
❌ COMMON TRAPNo, I have no some questions.
✅ COSY CORRECTIONI have no questions. OR I don't have any questions.
📐 COSY FORMULAOption 1: Subject + have + no + [Noun]
Option 2: Subject + don't have + any + [Noun]
💡 MEMORY HOOKNo and some cancel each other out like oil and water. Never pair them together!
📝 QUICK QUIZ: Fill in the blank
"I checked my bag, but I have ___ snacks."
A. no some
B. any
C. no
Answer: C — Pick no + noun OR don't + verb + any + noun. Simple!

4. "I arrived the station" — Preposition Needs

SLIDE 04 / 05
❌ COMMON TRAPYesterday, I arrived the station at six. • I listened music.
✅ COSY CORRECTIONI arrived at the station at six. • I listened to music.
📐 COSY FORMULASubject + arrive at + [Building / Station] OR arrive in + [City / Country]
💡 MEMORY HOOKArrive at for places with walls or boundaries (stations, offices); arrive in for cities & countries (Paris, France).
📝 QUICK QUIZ: Which is correct?
A. We discussed about the problem.
B. We discussed the problem.
C. We listened the problem.
Answer: B — Learn verbs with their target prepositions attached: arrive at, listen to, discuss (no about).

5. "It depends of you" — The Preposition Trap

SLIDE 05 / 05
❌ COMMON TRAPIt depends of you.
✅ COSY CORRECTIONIt depends on you.
📐 COSY FORMULASubject + depend on + [Person / Factor / Situation]
💡 MEMORY HOOKMany languages use 'of' or 'from' for dependence, but in English you rest on something for support — hence depend ON!
📝 QUICK QUIZ: Fill in the blank
"I'm interested ___ learning French."
A. on
B. in
C. at
Answer: B — Don't memorize single words—memorize pairs like depend on, interested in, good at.
