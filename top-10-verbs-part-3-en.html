Deep Dive: Top 10 English Verbs (Part 3)

  • 1. Choose / Buy / Pay: Selection and commerce. • Usage & Frames: 'I choose a book', 'I buy groceries', 'I pay by card'. • Useful Phrases: 'choose wisely', 'buy online', 'pay attention', 'pay for dinner'.
  • 2. Remember / Forget: Memory and cognition. • Usage & Frames: 'I remember your name', 'Don't forget your keys!'. • Useful Phrases: 'remember to do', 'I forgot my password', 'hard to forget'.
  • 3. Feel: Physical and emotional state. • Usage & Frames: 'I feel happy', 'She feels cold', 'How do you feel?'. • Useful Phrases: 'feel like doing', 'feel well', 'feel tired'.
  • 4. Meet: Social interactions and introductions. • Usage & Frames: 'Nice to meet you', 'Let's meet at 5 PM', 'Meet my friend'. • Useful Phrases: 'meet up', 'meet new people', 'pleased to meet you'.
  • 5. Decide: Decision-making. • Usage & Frames: 'I decide to stay', 'Decide on a location'. • Important Note: We say 'solve/resolve a problem', NOT 'decide a problem'. • Useful Phrases: 'decide quickly', 'hard to decide', 'make up your mind'.
  • 6. Write / Read / Type: Textual expression and literacy. • Usage & Frames: 'Write an email', 'Read a book', 'Type a message'. • Useful Phrases: 'write down', 'read aloud', 'type fast'.
  • 7. Spend / Waste: Managing resources. • Usage & Frames: 'I spend money on books', 'Don't waste time!'. • Useful Phrases: 'spend time with family', 'waste energy', 'spend money on'.
  • 8. Play (all variations): Games, sports, instruments, roles. • Usage & Frames: 'Play football / games', 'Play the guitar / piano', 'Play a role in a film'. • Useful Phrases: 'play sports', 'play an instrument', 'play a key role'.
  • 9. Agree / Disagree: Consensus and opinion. • Usage & Frames: 'I agree with you', 'We agree on the plan', 'I disagree'. • Useful Phrases: 'totally agree', 'agree to differ', 'respectfully disagree'.
  • 10. Send / Get: Transmitting and receiving. • Usage & Frames: 'Send a text message', 'I get an email', 'Get home at 6 PM'. • Useful Phrases: 'send an email', 'get back', 'get to the station'.
