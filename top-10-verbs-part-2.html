🗺️ The 10 Essential Core Verbs (Part 2 Focus)

These 10 essential action & perception anchors power everyday speech across all 14 official languages:

1. Do / Make — Activities, homework vs creation.
2. Speak / Talk / Say / Tell — Communication, speaking, storytelling.
3. Look / Watch / See — Visual perception and attention.
4. Listen / Hear — Active listening vs hearing sounds.
5. Must / Have to — Necessity, obligations, duties.
6. Take / Give / Get — Exchange, obtaining, receiving.
7. Live — Dwelling, location, lifestyle.
8. Wait — Patience, waiting for transport/time.
9. Understand — Comprehension, active listening.
10. Ask / Answer — Questions, answers, requests.
