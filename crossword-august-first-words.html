THEMATIC CROSSWORD · A2–C1

The August Crossword: First Words

18 words this issue keeps using, fitted into one grid. Solve it out loud with a partner and you have rehearsed half the vocabulary of the month.

J Written by JY DM
📅 2026-08-01 ⏱️ 6 min read A2–C1

Every word in this grid comes from the pages around it: the classroom in the photographs, the first minute of a lesson, the courage it takes to say the wrong thing out loud, one breakfast from the Culinary Salon. Solve it with a pen and a partner — one reads the clue, the other says the answer first.

How to solve it aloud. Read the clue in the language you are learning. Say the answer out loud before you write a letter. If you are stuck, say the first sound and let your partner finish the word. The grid rewards the people who speak first and write second.

📌 This could be a good research question

Does solving a crossword aloud, with a partner, move a word into active use faster than copying it into a notebook? Run it for two weeks with your speaking club: half the group copies ten words, half solves the puzzle aloud, then both groups use the words in a five-minute conversation. Count how many each group actually says.

One last instruction for the classroom: do not hand out the key. Read the answers aloud, one number at a time, and ask the group to mark their own grid. The correction is the lesson.
