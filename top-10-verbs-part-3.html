🗺️ The 10 Essential Core Verbs (Part 3 Focus)

These 10 essential verb clusters power everyday decisions, commerce, cognition, emotions, and interactions across all 14 official languages:

1. Choose & Buy & Pay — Selection, shopping, transactions by card/cash.
2. Remember & Forget — Memory retention, recalling names & items.
3. Feel — Expressing feelings, mood, cold/warmth, tiredness.
4. Meet — Social gatherings, meeting friends, introductions.
5. Decide — Choice & intentions ('decide to do' / 'decide on'; note: solve a problem).
6. Write & Read & Type — Literacy, messages, reading books, typing text.
7. Spend & Waste — Resource management with time and money.
8. Play (all variations) — Sports, board games, musical instruments, roles.
9. Agree & Disagree — Alignment, consensus, expressing different views.
10. Send & Get — Sending messages, receiving items, arriving at places.
