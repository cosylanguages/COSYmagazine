Welcome to COSYmagazine, the editorial corner of COSY Languages, a free site for learning 14 languages. Think of it as the notebook that sits next to the lessons: a place for ideas, stories, grammar curiosities and gentle advice for anyone learning a language. I'm JY DM, the founder of COSY, and I'm glad you are here.

Everything on COSY is built around one belief: speaking-first, immersion-based learning. You do not need to memorise abstract tables before you are allowed to talk. You connect with real people and say real things from day one, and the grammar follows. The magazine is where we think about that out loud.

How the magazine works

COSYmagazine is published in issues, one for each month, each with its own theme. Inside an issue, articles are grouped into desks, a bit like sections in a newspaper:

  • Front Page: editorials, introductions and the big ideas behind COSY.
  • Words: vocabulary, phrases and ways to say things more naturally.
  • Grammar: clear, friendly explanations and rules worth knowing.
  • COSYtalks: longer conversations about learning, the mind and teaching.
  • Saturday Mistakes: common errors, with a corrected sentence and a quick quiz.
  • Podcast: the articles as listening, for walks, buses and washing-up.

Four ways to read

Not everybody likes to read the same way, so each article can be opened in several modes.

  • 3D magazine spread: the default. Pages turn like a real magazine, with the handwritten notes in the margins.
  • Flat scroll: a simple, calm, one-column page, ideal for phones.
  • Presentation mode: add ?stage=1 to an article's address and it plays block by block, perfect for a class screen.
  • Teleprompter: add ?script=1 and you get the spoken script in large type, handy if you want to read an article aloud or record your own version.

The COSY ecosystem

The magazine is one part of a small family of free tools. COSYpractice is where you do the speaking, listening and reading exercises. COSYtools is a set of helpers for learners and teachers. Whenever an article mentions a phrase or a rule, you will usually find a link so you can try it straight away.

📌 Culture bite: why a magazine?

The word magazine comes from an Arabic word for a storehouse, and the idea of a publication as a storehouse of many different things passed into French and then English. That is exactly what we want here: a store of small, useful ideas that you can browse in any order, dip into for five minutes and come back to whenever you like.

❓ What does adding ?stage=1 to an article address do?

?stage=1 plays the article block by block in presentation mode. ?script=1 shows the teleprompter script instead.

Pick an issue, pick a desk, pick the way you like to read. And when something sparks an idea, say it out loud in the language you are learning. That is the whole method, really.
