Welcome to our September notebook special issue! Discover natural upgrades, stronger adjectives, and precise verb synonyms to overcome the intermediate plateau.

One of the most noticeable signs of reaching the "intermediate plateau" in English is relying on the modifier "very" to intensify everyday adjectives.

While saying "very big", "very good", or "very bad" is grammatically correct and understandable, overusing "very" makes your speech and writing sound repetitive. Transitioning to stronger, more expressive single-word adjectives immediately makes your English sound more natural, sophisticated, and precise.

📈 The Spectrum of Adjectives: From Baseline to Elite Level

To truly break through the intermediate plateau, you need to understand that vocabulary words aren't just synonyms—they sit on a spectrum of nuance, intensity, and context. Here is how to navigate from level 0 to elite vocabulary across major everyday adjective categories:

🌟 1. Praise & Quality ("Good")

📊 Visual Progression: Baseline ➔ Overused Chain vs. Smart Spectrum Upgrade
good➔very good➔extremely good➔super good
good➔enjoyable➔great➔excellent➔brilliant / masterful
  1. Level 0: Baseline (Good / Nice) — Functional, but generic and plain.
  2. Level 1: Slight Upgrade (Pleasant & Satisfactory) — Enjoyable (an enjoyable afternoon walk), Satisfying (a satisfying meal), Solid (a solid performance).
  3. Level 2: Strong & Impressive (Ditch "Very Good") — Great (a great effort), Impressive (impressive results), Remarkable (a remarkable talent), Superb (superb customer service).
  4. Level 3: Top Tier (Ditch "Extremely Good") — Excellent (an excellent contribution), Exceptional (exceptional service), Outstanding (an outstanding achievement), Top-notch (top-notch atmosphere).
  5. Level 4: Pinnacle / Elite (Ditch "Super Good") — Brilliant (a brilliant idea), Masterful (masterful storytelling), Phenomenal (a phenomenal performance), Flawless (flawless execution), Exquisite (exquisite craftsmanship).

⚠️ 2. Criticism & Negativity ("Bad")

📊 Visual Progression: Baseline ➔ Overused Chain vs. Smart Spectrum Upgrade
bad / poor➔very bad➔extremely bad➔super bad
subpar➔disappointing➔awful➔atrocious➔disastrous / abysmal
  1. Level 0: Baseline (Bad / Poor) — Basic negative assessment.
  2. Level 1: Moderate Flaw (Subpar & Unsatisfactory) — Disappointing (a disappointing score), Inadequate (inadequate preparation), Subpar (subpar quality).
  3. Level 2: Strong Negativity (Ditch "Very Bad") — Awful (awful weather), Terrible (terrible service), Dreadful (a dreadful mistake).
  4. Level 3: High Impact / Severe (Ditch "Extremely Bad") — Atrocious (atrocious conditions), Abysmal (abysmal turnout), Appalling (appalling behavior).
  5. Level 4: Catastrophic / Crisis Level — Disastrous (a disastrous failure), Catastrophic (catastrophic breakdown), Deplorable (deplorable hygiene).

🏔️ 3. Size & Scale ("Big")

📊 Visual Progression: Baseline ➔ Overused Chain vs. Smart Spectrum Upgrade
big / large➔very big➔extremely big➔super big
big➔sizable➔huge➔gigantic➔colossal / monumental
  1. Level 0: Baseline (Big / Large) — Standard dimension indicator.
  2. Level 1: Moderate Scale — Sizable (a sizable investment), Substantial (substantial progress), Considerable (considerable experience).
  3. Level 2: Strong Scale (Ditch "Very Big") — Huge (a huge milestone), Enormous (enormous pressure), Massive (a massive success).
  4. Level 3: Top Tier Scale — Gigantic (a gigantic structure), Immense (immense pride), Tremendous (tremendous growth).
  5. Level 4: Astronomical / Elite Scale — Colossal (a colossal effort), Monumental (a monumental achievement), Astronomical (astronomical costs).

✨ 4. Aesthetics & Engagement ("Beautiful" & "Interesting")

📊 Visual Progression: Baseline ➔ Overused Chain vs. Smart Spectrum Upgrade
pretty➔very beautiful➔very interesting➔super interesting
attractive➔gorgeous➔breathtaking➔captivating➔mesmerizing / enthralling
  1. Level 0: Baseline (Pretty / Interesting) — Common conversational fillers.
  2. Level 1: Pleasant Upgrade — Attractive (attractive design), Engaging (an engaging topic), Appealing (an appealing option).
  3. Level 2: Strong Appeal (Ditch "Very Beautiful/Interesting") — Gorgeous (gorgeous view), Fascinating (a fascinating book), Stunning (a stunning gown).
  4. Level 3: Deep Impact — Breathtaking (breathtaking landscape), Captivating (a captivating speaker), Intriguing (an intriguing mystery).
  5. Level 4: Spellbinding / Elite Level — Mesmerizing (a mesmerizing sunset), Enthralling (an enthralling performance), Radiant (a radiant smile).

⚡ 5. Emotion & Energy ("Angry" & "Tired")

📊 Visual Progression: Baseline ➔ Overused Chain vs. Smart Spectrum Upgrade
angry / tired➔very angry➔very tired➔extremely tired
annoyed / weary➔furious➔exhausted➔outraged➔seething / utterly spent
  1. Level 0: Baseline (Angry / Tired) — Everyday state descriptions.
  2. Level 1: Mild Intensity — Annoyed / Irritated (annoyed by delay), Weary (a weary traveler), Drained (feeling drained).
  3. Level 2: High Intensity (Ditch "Very Angry/Tired") — Furious (furious at the decision), Exhausted (exhausted after the marathon), Livid (livid about the breach).
  4. Level 3: Extreme Intensity — Outraged (outraged public), Worn-out (completely worn-out), Enraged (enraged crowd).
  5. Level 4: Maximum Threshold — Seething (seething with anger), Utterly Spent (utterly spent after work).

🌐 Multilingual Adaptations for FR, RU, IT & EL Learners

In every target language, intermediate learners fall into the habit of relying on a single general intensifier (très, очень, molto, πολύ). Here is how to upgrade your adjectives in French, Russian, Italian, and Greek:

🇫🇷 French (très + adjectif → adjectifs forts)

très grand → immense, gigantesque
très bon → excellent, remarquable, formidable
très fatigué → épuisé, exténué
très beau → superbe, magnifique, splendide
très intéressant → passionnant, captivant

🇷🇺 Russian (очень + прилагательное → сильные прилагательные)

очень большой → огромный, гигантский
очень хороший → отличный, великолепный, превосходный
очень усталый → изнуренный, обессиленный
очень красивый → прекрасный, изумительный
очень интересный → захватывающий, увлекательный

🇮🇹 Italian (molto + aggettivo → aggettivi forti)

molto grande → immenso, gigantesco
molto buono → eccellente, squisito, ottimo
molto stanco → esausto, sinito
molto bello → bellissimo, magnifico, splendido
molto interessante → affascinante, coinvolgente

🇬🇷 Greek (πολύ + επίθετο → ισχυρά επίθετα)

πολύ μεγάλος → τεράστιος, γιγάντιος
πολύ καλός → εξαιρετικός, υπέροχος
πολύ κουρασμένος → εξαντλημένος, κατάκοπος
πολύ ωραίος / όμορφος → πανέμορφος, καταπληκτικός
πολύ ενδιαφέρων → συναρπαστικός, καθηλωτικός

Summary: Practicing Strong Adjectives

When learning these new words, try substituting them directly into your daily journal or speaking practice. For instance, instead of saying "I had a very busy day and I am very tired", say "I had a hectic day and I am exhausted." Notice how much more vivid and clear your sentence becomes!
