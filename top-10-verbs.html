🗺️ The 10 Essential Core Verbs (Part 1 Focus)

These 10 essential action anchors power everyday speech across all 14 official languages:

1. To Be — Existence, identity, states, age.
2. To Have — Possession, physical states, obligations.
3. To Think — Expressing opinions, beliefs, thoughts.
4. To Know — Facts, skills, familiarity.
5. To Like — Preferences, tastes, enjoyment.
6. To Want / Would like — Desires, orders, polite requests.
7. To Go / Be going to — Movement, travel, future intention.
8. To Can — Modal ability, possibility, permission.
9. To Eat — Meals, food consumption, dining out.
10. To Drink — Beverages, hydration, social drinks.

# Verb Anchor Core Meaning & Function Key Example Sentence
1To BeExistence, identity, states, age, locationI am ready. / She is a doctor.
2To HavePossession, relationships, physical statesI have a phone. / She has brown eyes.
3To ThinkOpinions, thoughts, beliefsI think it is a great idea.
4To KnowFacts, familiarity, skills (know how to)I know the answer. / She knows how to swim.
5To LikePreferences, tastes, enjoymentI like learning languages.
6To Want / Would LikeDesires, ordering, polite requestsI want to learn English. / I'd like a coffee.
7To Go / Be Going ToMovement, travel, future intentionI go to work by train. / I'm going to travel.
8To CanModal ability, requests, permissionI can speak English. / Can you help me?
9To EatMeals, food consumption, dining outI eat fresh fruit every morning.
10To DrinkBeverages, hydration, social drinksI drink two litres of water daily.
