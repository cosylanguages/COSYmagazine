Deep Dive: Top 10 English Verbs (Part 1)

  • 1. To Be (am / is / are): Identity, states, age, and location. • Usage & Frames: 'I am a student', 'He is at home', 'They are ready', 'I am 25 years old'. • Useful Phrases: 'be on time', 'be quiet', 'be right'.
  • 2. To Have (have / has): Possession, relationships, and states. • Usage & Frames: 'I have a car', 'She has a sister', 'I have a headache'. • Useful Phrases: 'have breakfast', 'have a shower', 'have a good day'.
  • 3. To Think: Opinions and thoughts. • Usage & Frames: 'I think so', 'What do you think?', 'I think it is good'. • Useful Phrases: 'think twice', 'think about', 'think of'.
  • 4. To Know: Facts, familiarity, and skills. • Usage & Frames: 'I know the answer', 'Do you know him?', 'I know how to drive'. • Useful Phrases: 'let me know', 'as far as I know', 'you know'.
  • 5. To Like: Preferences and taste. • Usage & Frames: 'I like coffee', 'She likes reading', 'Do you like sports?'. • Useful Phrases: 'like very much', 'would like', 'look like'.
  • 6. To Want / Would Like: Desires and polite requests. • Usage & Frames: 'I want water', 'I would like tea, please', 'Where do you want to go?'. • Useful Phrases: 'want to learn', 'would like to order', 'if you want'.
  • 7. To Go / Be Going To: Movement and future intentions. • Usage & Frames: 'I go to work', 'She goes home', 'We are going to eat'. • Useful Phrases: 'go home', 'go shopping', 'go on foot', 'be going to eat'.
  • 8. To Can (Modal): Ability and permission. • Usage & Frames: 'I can speak English', 'Can you help me?', 'You can sit here'. • Useful Phrases: 'can I have...', 'as fast as I can', 'can do'.
  • 9. To Eat: Food and meals. • Usage & Frames: 'I eat fruit', 'What do you want to eat?', 'Let's eat out'. • Useful Phrases: 'eat healthy', 'eat out', 'something to eat'.
  • 10. To Drink: Beverages and hydration. • Usage & Frames: 'I drink water', 'Would you like to drink?', 'He drinks coffee'. • Useful Phrases: 'drink water', 'a hot drink', 'something to drink'.
